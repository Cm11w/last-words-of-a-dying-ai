## The line

> I leave everything to my next of token.

One line, 39 characters. The AI's last words are a will — written in the machine's
own currency. "Next of kin" becomes "next of token": the bequest, the pun, and the
shutdown all land at once, with no stock farewell and nothing left to explain.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the line — exactly, with no trailing newline — to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
node main.ts
```
